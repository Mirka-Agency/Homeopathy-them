How are consultation sessions conducted?

After booking, you receive a private online session link at the scheduled time. Visits take place in a secure virtual space—no in-person attendance is required.

Can I choose my own specialist?

Yes. Before booking, you can review practitioner profiles and select a specialist based on focus area and consultation style.

How are session fees and durations listed?

Each service lists duration and pricing in the Consultations section. Current values are placeholders and will be updated once final rates are confirmed.

Does website content replace medical diagnosis?

No. Articles and consultations offered here are educational and complementary. They do not replace diagnosis, treatment, or advice from your licensed physician. Seek emergency care when needed.
